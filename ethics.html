The ACM Code of Ethics

The Association for Computing Machinery (ACM) provides a framework for ethical decision-making in the IT profession. Key principles include:

  • Contribute to society and human well-being: Acknowledging that all people are stakeholders in computing.
  • Avoid harm: Minimizing negative consequences, including loss of information and property damage.
  • Be honest and trustworthy: Being transparent about system capabilities and limitations.
